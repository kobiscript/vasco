Technical detail belongs inside the game.

We connect individual creativity with intelligent movement and collective responsibility. Players learn patterns without having every answer scripted for them. They are encouraged to take risks without losing purpose and to understand not only what to do, but when, how, and why.

Mastering the basics

THE GAME GETS FASTER.
THE FOUNDATION STAYS.

The basics are not something players outgrow. They are the skills players depend on as space closes, pressure rises, and the next action arrives sooner.

01

Control creates options

A purposeful first touch helps a player protect the ball, escape pressure, or prepare the next action. Progress can be as simple and important as receiving cleanly in a difficult moment.

02

Awareness gives technique purpose

Looking before receiving helps players recognize space, pressure, and passing options. Technique becomes valuable when the player understands when to use it.

03

Fundamentals strengthen the team

Accurate passing, receiving with either foot, and moving to offer support allow teammates to keep the ball, solve pressure, and progress together.

The game, frame by frame

THE MOMENT IS SMALL.
THE PICTURE IS BIG.

Futsal compresses space and time. Players learn to collect information early, act with intention, and stay connected to the next moment.

01
Before the ball

SEE THE NEXT PICTURE.

Scan for space, pressure, teammates, and the direction of the next action before receiving.

02
In possession

MAKE THE TOUCH MEAN SOMETHING.

Control, protect, combine, or eliminate pressure with a decision that moves the moment forward.

03
After the action

MOVE AGAIN. STAY IN THE GAME.

Offer support, create a new angle, and react immediately when possession changes.

Vasco Da Lor development pathway

THREE TIERS.
ONE SHARED PURPOSE.

Age guides the starting point. Coaching reflects the player's experience, confidence, current ability, and individual development.

01 · ADVANCED DEVELOPMENTAGES 13-17

READ THE GAME.
EXECUTE WITH PURPOSE.

Connect technical quality with tactical understanding and take responsibility within a shared style of play.

  • Technical execution under pressure
  • Pattern recognition and movement
  • Defensive organization
  • Decision-making and leadership
TRAINING APPROACH

Competitive small-sided games, realistic match scenarios, and technical work connected to team principles.

02 · CONNECTING SKILLSAGES 10-12

BUILD THE SKILL.
UNDERSTAND THE CHOICE.

Make the fundamentals dependable and understand how individual actions connect with teammates.

  • Technical consistency with both feet
  • Awareness and decisions
  • Movement off the ball
  • Individual and small-group defending
TRAINING APPROACH

Ball work, 1v1, 2v1, 2v2, and small-sided games that give players clear problems to solve.

03 · FOUNDATIONAGES 7-9

ENJOY THE BALL.
EXPLORE THE GAME.

Build enjoyment, confidence, and a strong relationship with the ball without complex tactical instruction.

  • Ball familiarity with both feet
  • Balance and movement foundations
  • Simple decisions in space
  • Playing together and basic restarts
TRAINING APPROACH

Short activities, plenty of ball contact, 1v1 challenges, and simple small-sided games.
